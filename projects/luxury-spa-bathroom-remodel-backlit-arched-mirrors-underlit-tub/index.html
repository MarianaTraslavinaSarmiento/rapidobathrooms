Luxury Spa Bathroom Remodel: From Rough Build to a Glowing Hotel-Style Retreat

This project is a true before-and-after transformation—taking a raw construction space and turning it into a warm, modern spa bathroom with dramatic ambient lighting, sculptural details, and premium finishes. The final result feels like a boutique hotel suite: calm, architectural, and intentionally designed down to the smallest line.

The Tub Wall Feature: Soft Texture + Hidden Glow

The freestanding tub becomes the centerpiece, framed by a textured feature wall and a glowing recessed niche detail that reads like art. The underlighting along the tub platform creates a floating effect and instantly makes the room feel high-end.

Highlights:
  • ✓ Freestanding soaking tub as the main focal point
  • ✓ Underlit platform for a luxury “floating” look
  • ✓ Recessed accent niche with warm illumination for depth and drama
Floating Double Vanity with Vessel Sinks

The vanity wall is designed to feel clean and elevated: a floating cabinet with warm wood texture, paired with double vessel sinks and a continuous counter for a streamlined, modern profile.

What stands out:
  • ✓ Floating double vanity for an airy, modern silhouette
  • ✓Double vessel sinks for a designer, boutique feel
  • ✓Under-cabinet lighting that adds depth and nighttime usability
Backlit Arched Mirrors That Define the Mood

These mirrors are more than functional—they’re the signature element. The arched shape softens the room’s geometry while the backlighting creates a soft glow that makes everything feel calmer and more refined.

Finishing touches
  • ✓ LED backlit arched mirrors for soft, flattering light
  • ✓ Symmetry that makes the vanity wall feel intentional
  • ✓ A warm halo effect that turns the room into a spa at night
