Moody Spa Bathroom Remodel: From Construction Shell to Boutique Retreat

This project focuses on one thing—and does it exceptionally well: a streamlined, modern walk-in shower built around oversized gray stone-look tile. From the floating bench to the long linear drain, every detail was designed to feel minimal, easy to maintain, and quietly luxurious. The before-and-during photos show the full transformation—from waterproofing and layout prep to a crisp finished space.

Built for Calm: Oversized Tile That Makes the Room Feel Bigger

The large-format wall tile reduces grout lines and creates a continuous, architectural look. The subtle horizontal veining adds movement without looking busy—perfect for a shower that feels clean and timeless.

Highlights:
  • ✓ Large-format gray stone-look tile for a seamless, upscale finish
  • ✓ Minimal grout lines for easier cleaning and a modern aesthetic
  • ✓ Consistent wraparound tile layout that feels intentional and balanced
Floating Bench: Comfort Without Visual Clutter

Instead of a bulky built-in, the floating bench keeps the floor open and the silhouette light. It’s practical for shaving, relaxing, or setting essentials—without interrupting the sleek tilework.

What it adds:
  • ✓ Modern “floating” look with clear floor space underneath
  • ✓Functional seating that doesn’t overwhelm the shower footprint
  • ✓Clean edges that match the minimalist vibe of the project
