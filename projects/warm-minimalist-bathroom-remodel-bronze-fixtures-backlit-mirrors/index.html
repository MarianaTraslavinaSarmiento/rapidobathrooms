Warm Minimalist Bathroom Remodel with Bronze Fixtures & Backlit Mirrors

This project is all about calm, modern minimalism with a warmer edge—smooth light-toned walls, clean planes, and rich bronze hardware that adds depth without clutter. The lighting is built into the architecture, creating a soft glow around key features like the shower niche and the tall mirrors above the vanity.

A Walk-In Shower with a Clean, Built-In Look

The shower keeps everything streamlined: a rain shower head paired with a matching handheld set, plus a recessed niche for storage that stays visually quiet. The niche includes integrated lighting, creating a subtle highlight without adding extra fixtures.

Floating Double Vanity with Backlit Mirror Lighting
