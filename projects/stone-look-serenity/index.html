Stone-Look Serenity

This modern bathroom transformation balances a calm gray stone-look palette with bold black details. Large-format wall tiles keep the space clean and seamless, while the black shower base and glass enclosure sharpen the look. The shower features a rain head and handheld set, plus a recessed niche with a bright frosted/illuminated finish. A floating black vanity with a crisp white integrated sink completes the minimal, high-contrast design.

A recessed niche provides practical storage and stands out with a bright, frosted/illuminated appearance that adds a subtle focal point. The shower setup includes a large round rain showerhead paired with a handheld wand on a vertical rail, giving the shower wall a streamlined, modern profile. Outside the shower, a floating matte black vanity reinforces the bold contrast, topped with a white integrated sink and a sleek single-handle faucet in a brushed/steel tone. A black towel radiator complements the darker elements and keeps the design consistent from wall to floor.
