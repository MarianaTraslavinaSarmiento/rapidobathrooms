Modern Marble-Look Bathroom Remodel with Black-Framed Glass Shower

This project showcases a full transformation from an unfinished space to a bright, modern bathroom with a clean, high-end look. The finished design highlights large marble-look wall tile for a sleek, seamless feel, paired with a crisp white vanity and a modern mirror with a dark frame. The walk-in shower features a black-framed glass enclosure, matching dark fixtures, and a recessed shower niche for practical storage. The result is a polished, contemporary bathroom that feels open, fresh, and elevated—plus an organized adjacent closet area with built-in shelving and a cohesive transition into the bedroom.
